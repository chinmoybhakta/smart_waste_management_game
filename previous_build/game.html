How to Play

  • Goal: Get the Environmental Pollution Index (EPI) below 30% before the timer hits zero.
  • Citizens wander in — happy ones (mood 70+) walk their waste to a bin; unhappy ones litter the street.
  • Give Reward (Tk 1,000) temporarily lifts everyone's mood so they use the bins.
  • Buy Bin (Tk 1,500) lets you place a smart bin on any sidewalk tile, up to 3 total.
  • Click an already-placed bin to open its upgrade panel and raise its capacity.
  • Launch Ad Campaign (Tk 500) also boosts civic mood city-wide for a while.
  • Empty Bins moves collected waste into your warehouse.
  • B2B Sales opens a negotiation with a corporate buyer for your warehouse stock — accept, deny for a new offer, or close.
  • Watch the EPI gauge in the bottom panel — it needs to stay under the gold line by the time the clock runs out.
