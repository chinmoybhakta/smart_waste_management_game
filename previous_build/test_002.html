HOW TO PLAY

  • Clear all five Bangladesh stages in order: School/College, University, Garments & Office, Densely Populated, and Polluted Riverside Rural.
  • Each stage begins with open trash. Bring the Environmental Pollution Index to exactly 0% before the timer ends.
  • Build smart bins on highlighted sidewalk/placement zones. Click a placed bin to upgrade it up to Level 5 for larger capacity.
  • Public Revenue Share controls behavior. High public share raises mood and makes NPCs use bins. Low share increases frustration and littering.
  • Reward Drive temporarily motivates citizens and activates volunteer litter pickup. Ad Campaign gives a smaller city-wide mood boost.
  • Empty Bins transfers sorted material to the warehouse and writes an intake entry to the local ledger.
  • Use B2B Recycling Market to compare manufacturer offers. Plastic and E-Waste have high fair values, so reject exploitative low-ball bids.
  • Your net sale cash equals the negotiated gross amount minus the public revenue share. The public share improves civic trust.
